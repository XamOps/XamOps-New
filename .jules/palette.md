## 2026-04-15 - [Topbar & AccountSelector ARIA/Tooltips]
**Learning:** Adding accessible tooltips and ARIA attributes to common navigation elements (Topbar and Sidebar) significantly improves the keyboard and screen reader experience. Using a centralized `TooltipWrapper` ensures consistency.
**Action:** Always check icon-only buttons for ARIA labels and wrap them in `TooltipWrapper` when possible.
