  drained off these nodes is the downstream fit-simulator per-pod refusal (pvc-volume /
  statefulset / single-replica / PDB) — i.e. the SAME incidental net as G6, NOT a
  node-level DB guard. The optimizer itself has already (incorrectly) nominated the node.
