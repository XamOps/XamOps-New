  - xamops_drain_budget_inflight: 0 on both replicas => no drain ever in flight.
  - sum(xamops_leader)         : 1 => single leader throughout (no split-brain).

    original (Oct-25) start times EXCEPT pg-cnpg-2 and mongo-1, whose newer start times
    match THIS experiment's Chaos pod-kill/failover (not a XamOps eviction).

Interpretation: the DB fixtures stayed up with no XamOps-caused disruption; the only
restarts are the ones this experiment injected (CNPG primary kill, Mongo RS re-election),
and the CNPG/StatefulSet controllers recovered them from their PVCs. All 7 DB pods ended
Running (E5-stateful/95-db-pods-recovered.txt).
