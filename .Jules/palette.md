## 2026-05-14 - Standardizing Accessible Icon Buttons
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons in the top navigation require both a `TooltipWrapper` for visual context and a matching `aria-label` for screen readers to ensure parity between visual and assistive experiences.
**Action:** Always wrap interactive icons in `TooltipWrapper` and apply `focus-visible` ring styles to satisfy both accessibility and UX delight requirements.
